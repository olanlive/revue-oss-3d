GPL Blender add-on: select edges and change their segment count with the mouse wheel while keeping UVs, seams, sharp edges and vertex colours. Shift smooths the curve, Ctrl spaces points evenly. Released on Oct 11, 2026, already close to 900 downloads.

GPL Blender ↔ 3D-Coat bridge (AppLink protocol): predictable two-way model sync, without baking or scene surgery. 0.2.0 (Sept 24) tightens the bridge; one-click install. Niche but useful if 3D-Coat is in your sculpt/retopo pipeline.
