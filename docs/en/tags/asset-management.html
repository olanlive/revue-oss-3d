RealIndex 1.1.0

GPL Blender 5.2 extension that turns a folder into a live asset library: drop in a Sketchfab model, an ambientCG or Poly Haven texture or an HDRI (zipped or not) and it shows up in the Asset Browser with a thumbnail, no manual import. v1.1.0 on Oct 17, 2026.

AYON Core 1.10

The open-source AYON pipeline (OpenPype’s successor) adds a Lists category to its Browser: entity lists and review sessions grouped in folders, to filter versions of a shot or task. 1.10.0 on Oct 7, 2026, 1.10.1 fix on Oct 13.

Texture Wrangler 1.0.0

GPL Blender add-on that detects every map (normal, roughness, AO…) whatever its naming, then copies and renames the textures into clean per-material folders without touching the originals. Released on Oct 11, 2026.

Minimalist Pipeline 1.0.14

Lightweight file pipeline inside Blender (GPL): projects, versions, notes/retakes, read-only sessions and a render farm on a shared drive (local JSON). Version 1.0.14 (Oct 15) adds farm tracking to monitoring and sequence previews.
