SplatCraft 0.1.3

SplatCraft 0.1.3 (Nov 22, 2026): local, MIT-licensed Gaussian splatting studio. Drop photos or videos: frames extracted and filtered, COLMAP alignment (with manual matches), Brush training with live preview, then export. Early prototype, tested on macOS.

Scan3D

Scan3D (19 Nov 2026): MIT Windows pipeline that turns photos or a phone video into a Gaussian splat, a textured mesh and a collision mesh in one coordinate system, via Spirula Studio and COLMAP, with a web UI and no cloud.
