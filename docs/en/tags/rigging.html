B-Bone Gizmos 1.0.0

GPL Blender extension by Tal Hershkovich, extracted from AnimToolbox: a Pose Mode gizmo tool to shape bendy bones like Bézier handles (location, rotation, scale). Cited at the Oct 15, 2026 Animation & Rigging meeting as a reference for a native version.

SMIRK 1.0.1

GPL Blender add-on (5.2+) for carving mouths, eyes and other stylised openings into a mesh from an edge loop, a ribbon or Grease Pencil — the “boolean mouth”, built for production. Released on Oct 13, 2026.

Motion2MixamoRig (Blender extension)

Free tool (Apache-2.0) that copies the motion of a filmed person onto a Mixamo-rigged character and outputs a skinned GLB. An experimental Blender 4.2+ extension runs the pipeline from Blender. Featured on X on Oct 13.

Shape Key & Vertex Groups Utilities Enhanced 2.0.0

Free Blender add-on that sorts shape keys into folders without changing their real order, with batch operations, transfer between meshes and tools for vertex groups and UV maps. 2.0.0 came out on Oct 13 on Blender Extensions.

ChainCut – Bone Chain Slicer

Blender 5.2+ add-on (GPL-3.0) that cuts bones into 2 to 8 connected segments, keeping clean names and redistributing vertex group weights, unlike the native Subdivide. Version 0.1.0, created on Oct 12.

EpicFigRig 2.0 (Blender 5 fork)

GPL-3.0 fork of the EpicFigRig LEGO minifig auto-rig, compatible with Blender 4.0 and 5.0 while the original breaks in 5.0: one-click rigging of Mecabricks exports, IK/FK, capes and accessories. Release 2.0 published in May 2026.
