PostProject 0.6.0-alpha.1 (+ Blender VSE extension)

Media identity layer for post-production: stable asset IDs, provenance and deterministic relinking by content fingerprint. 0.6.0-alpha.1 came out on Oct 4, with drivers for Blender Sequencer, Kdenlive, Natron, OBS and Ardour.

ZeroGeometry Kit 1.0.1

GPL Blender add-on: one-click vertical reframing (9:16 / 4:5 / 1:1) with auto-follow, beat sync, loop doctor and wedge renders. First public release, for adapting a 16:9 animation.
