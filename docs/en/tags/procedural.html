RG Houdini Bridge 1.0.0

RG Houdini Bridge 1.0.0 (Nov 25, 2026): GPL Blender extension that turns Houdini nodes and your own HDAs into live, non-destructive modifiers that update when the mesh changes, with no export step. Needs a local Houdini (tested with Indie, Windows, Blender 5.2).

OVV Orient Vector Viewer 1.0.2

OVV 1.0.2 (first public release, Nov 21, 2026): GPL Blender 5.2+ add-on that visualizes vectors and orientations in Geometry Nodes, Houdini style. Press V to wire the viewer; arrows, bases or cubes, plus quaternion and matrix math nodes.

Found via: BlenderArtists — OVV: Orient Vector Viewer

Harappa 1.2.1

Harappa 1.2.1 (16 Nov 2026): a GPL Blender add-on that turns terrain into an anime-style meadow in EEVEE (painted grass, flowers, trees, cel-shaded sky). 1.2 adds ponds, streams, waterfalls, rocks and weather; 1.2.1 makes the water flow again.

GH Stack (Grasshopper ↔ Blender)

Blender extension (GPL-3.0) that drives Grasshopper definitions as stackable modifiers alongside Geometry Nodes, with animatable parameters, via WebSocket to Rhino 8. v0.1.x demo, created on Oct 12.
