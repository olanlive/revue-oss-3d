PolyTailor 1.1.1

PolyTailor (published Nov 13, 1.1.1 on Nov 20, 2026): GPL Blender retopology and modeling extension for Edit Mode. Four tools on a W wheel: Build draws faces and quad strips, Fill caps holes with clean quads, plus Relax and Tweak; Topo mode snaps to the source model.

AssetBridge (Nov 16–17, 2026): local open-source (MIT) asset browser for Blender and Cinema 4D. It indexes your existing folders (Megascans, Poly Haven, kits, Gaussian splats…), shows a thumbnail grid and 3D previews, and sends an asset in one click with its materials.
