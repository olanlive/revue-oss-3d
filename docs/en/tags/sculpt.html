Sculpt Transform Plus 0.1.0

Sculpt Transform Plus 0.1.0 (Nov 20, 2026): GPL Blender 5.3+ extension that keeps the Transform gizmo aligned with the pivot in Sculpt Mode. Pivot placed on the surface (Shift + right click), remembered per object, local axis locks and native undo.
